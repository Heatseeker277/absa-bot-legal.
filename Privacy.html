Privacy Policy Last updated: July 29, 2026 This Privacy Policy applies to the Discord bots ABSA and ABSA Drift, collectively referred to as “the Bots.” This policy explains what information the Bots access, process, and transfer while providing their features. 1. Information the Bots Process The Bots may process limited information required to connect the ABSA Assetto Corsa game servers with Discord. This may include: Assetto Corsa in-game usernames. In-game chat messages. Game server information. Discord server, channel, and message identifiers. Discord usernames, user IDs, roles, and permissions when required for Bot commands. Commands and interactions submitted directly to the Bots. 2. Assetto Corsa Chat Messages The Bots may receive chat messages sent by players on ABSA Assetto Corsa servers. These messages may be transferred and posted to designated channels in the ABSA Discord server. A transferred message may include the player’s in-game username, the message content, and information identifying the game server it came from. Players should understand that messages written in the Assetto Corsa game chat may therefore become visible to Discord users who have access to the relevant Discord channel. The Bots do not use these messages for advertising, marketing, or user profiling. 3. Discord Message Access The Bots do not read or monitor general Discord message content. The Bots may only access information provided directly through Discord commands, interactions, buttons, or other Bot features. Messages transferred from Assetto Corsa are posted to Discord by the Bots but are not collected from existing Discord conversations. 4. Data Storage The Bots do not maintain a permanent database containing personal user data or complete chat histories. Assetto Corsa messages may be processed temporarily while they are being transferred to Discord. After a message is posted to Discord, it may remain stored in the relevant Discord channel according to Discord’s systems, the server’s settings, and the actions of Discord server administrators. Temporary technical information may also appear in short-lived system or error logs for troubleshooting, security, and maintaining the Bots. 5. How Information Is Used Information processed by the Bots is used only to: Transfer Assetto Corsa chat messages to Discord. Display game server activity. Provide Bot commands and features. Perform moderation or administrative functions. Maintain security and prevent abuse. Diagnose technical problems. Keep the Bots and game server integrations operational. 6. Sharing of Information The Bots do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Assetto Corsa chat messages may be shared with members of the ABSA Discord server by posting them in designated Discord channels. Information may also be processed through Discord and the infrastructure used to operate the Bots and Assetto Corsa servers. Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, protect users, prevent abuse, or maintain the security of the Bots and servers. 7. Discord The Bots operate through Discord and rely on Discord’s platform and API. Messages posted to Discord are processed and stored by Discord according to Discord’s own Privacy Policy and systems. This Privacy Policy only covers data handling performed by ABSA and ABSA Drift. It does not control how Discord independently collects, stores, or processes information. 8. Data Retention and Deletion The Bots do not permanently store Assetto Corsa chat messages in a separate Bot database. Messages transferred to Discord may remain visible in Discord until they are deleted by an authorized moderator, administrator, or Discord. Users may contact the ABSA administration team to request the removal of a transferred message. Requests will be reviewed, but removal may not always be possible where the information has already been copied, quoted, logged, or shared by other users. Removing the Bots from a Discord server prevents them from posting new information to that server. 9. User Responsibilities Users should not submit sensitive personal information through Assetto Corsa game chat or Bot commands. This includes passwords, payment information, private addresses, personal identification numbers, or other confidential information. Users are responsible for the information they voluntarily submit through the game chat. 10. Security Reasonable measures are used to protect the Bots, game servers, and related systems from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. However, no internet-based service can guarantee complete security. 11. Children’s Privacy The Bots and ABSA game servers are intended only for users who are permitted to use Discord and Assetto Corsa under the applicable platform rules and laws. The Bots are not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. 12. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bots, game server integrations, data practices, applicable laws, or Discord requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page will show when the policy was last revised. 13.
